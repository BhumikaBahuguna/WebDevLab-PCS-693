Outpatient Department (OPD)

The Outpatient Department (OPD) at City Care Hospital offers expert medical consultations without the need for hospitalization. Our highly qualified doctors provide accurate diagnoses, preventive care, and personalized treatment plans to ensure the best health outcomes.

Our OPD Services

General Medicine

Comprehensive health checkups and treatment for common illnesses.

Pediatrics

Specialized care for infants, children, and adolescents.

Orthopedics

Advanced treatment for bone, joint, and muscle conditions.

Dermatology

Expert care for skin, hair, and cosmetic concerns.
